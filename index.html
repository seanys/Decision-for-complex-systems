AI for Real-Time Decision-Making in Complex Systems

With growing automation, digitalization and connectivity, real-time decision-making in complex systems (e.g., transportation, energy and cloud computing) is becoming both feasible and necessary. How can we use AI to make efficient, reliable and interpretable decisions in these large-scale complex systems?
